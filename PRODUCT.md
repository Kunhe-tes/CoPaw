# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CoPaw has two primary product surfaces:

1. **Management Console**: used by operators, administrators, and AI-workflow owners who configure providers, models, tenants, channels, scheduled tasks, runtime settings, and operational policies. They usually arrive with a concrete administrative job, need to scan Chinese and English labels quickly, and expect the interface to behave like dependable enterprise software rather than a promotional site.
2. **Conversation Workspace**: used by people working directly with AI through a Claw-like chat interface. They need a focused, high-trust workspace for messages, generated artifacts, tool execution, task progress, attachments, model context, and session history.

## Product Purpose

CoPaw exists to support both AI operations management and active AI-assisted work.

The Management Console makes AI operations observable, configurable, and controllable across embedded host contexts. Success means users can understand current state, change configuration safely, and recover from errors.

The Conversation Workspace helps users think, ask, delegate, inspect tool execution, review generated outputs, and continue work across sessions. Success means the conversation, current task, and produced artifacts stay visually primary while surrounding navigation and management controls remain available without stealing attention.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, capable.

The interface should feel advanced without showing off: high-trust, quiet, dense enough for repeated operational work, and focused enough for long AI conversations.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS pages with oversized hero sections, purple-blue gradients, decorative blobs, and card piles.
- Marketing layouts that explain the product instead of letting users do their work.
- Over-branded dashboards that compete with the embedded host product.
- Chat pages where navigation, cards, examples, or decorative onboarding compete with the live conversation.
- Conversation UIs that bury generated files, tool progress, model context, or session continuity behind unclear secondary panels.
- Novel controls, decorative motion, hidden primary actions, and inconsistent button or form vocabulary.
- Fragile pages that only look good with perfect demo data.

## Design Principles

1. Task first, decoration last.
2. Earn trust through consistency, visible state, and recoverable actions.
3. Use density deliberately: compact, scannable, and never cramped.
4. Keep CoPaw embedded: white-first management surfaces, restrained accents, and no competing brand theater.
5. Keep conversation primary in the chat workspace: messages, current task, generated content, and execution feedback outrank decorative guidance.
6. Harden for real work: long text, CJK content, permissions, empty data, errors, loading, attachments, generated artifacts, and narrow host containers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Console UI should target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard accessibility for newly created or modified surfaces. Design work must preserve visible focus, non-color-only status communication, readable Chinese typography, reduced-motion respect, and usable states for disabled, unavailable, loading, and permission-limited actions.
